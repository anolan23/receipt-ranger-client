# Mascot: Pip the ranger

Pip is a small raccoon park ranger: a butter-yellow campaign hat with a coral band and a star, a sage neckerchief, a ringed tail and a receipt always in paw. Raccoons are tidy collectors who "wash" what they find, which makes a good metaphor for sorting messy receipts. Pip is friendly, a little earnest and never childish.

## Moods

- `happy`: greetings, empty states, the logo mark. Pairs with "Let's scan your first receipt!".
- `reading`: while a scan is processing; the receipt sways gently and the eyes look down. Pairs with "Reading receipt 2 of 4…".
- `celebrate`: once when a batch finishes, with sparkles twinkling around. Pairs with "Nice, 4 receipts scanned!".
- `oops`: a read error or a denied camera, with a worried brow and a sweat drop. Always followed by a way forward.

## Rules

- Draw Pip only from the shared SVG (160 by 160 viewBox, 2.5px `ink`-coloured outline, flat fills). Do not redraw, recolour or crop it other than the head-only logo crop.
- At most one Pip per screen, minimum 48px, on `surface-100`, `surface-raised` or a `blob-*` fill. Never over text, charts or the camera preview.
- Pip is never shown scolding about money. Spending increases get an amber chip, not a worried Pip.
